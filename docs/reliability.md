# Reliability metrics

Availability is served energy divided by required energy, expressed as a percentage. Unserved energy integrates the demand gap across quarter-hour steps. Outage duration sums intervals with a remaining gap. Critical availability compares protected critical energy served with the critical requirement. Renewable utilization divides renewable energy used by renewable energy produced. Paired comparisons use the same generated demand and renewable series. Metric differences are calculated from the run and are not hard-coded.
